# Charaka Sūtrasthāna 12 — Vātakalākalīya

## Research manifest

- Curriculum: **AyUG-SA1 / First Professional**
- Classical identity: Caraka Saṃhitā, Sūtrasthāna 12
- Title: **Vātakalākalīya (वातकलाकलीय)**
- Primary sequence source: Vedic Samhita, Caraka Sūtrasthāna 12
- Edition: SARIT IAST; Vedic Samhita states that its Devanagari is mechanically transliterated from SARIT.
- Source reports **22 passages, 16 in prose**.
- Canonical structure:
  - 12.1–12.14: prose
  - 12.15: prose marker followed by 12.15ab and 12.15cd verse-half records
  - 12.16: prose marker followed by 12.16ab and 12.16cd verse-half records
  - 12.17ab and 12.17cd: verse-half records
- Therefore: **16 prose + 6 verse-half = 22 passages**.
- Main themes: six vāta qualities; causes of vāta aggravation/pacification; vāta functions in body and world; clinical relevance of vāta knowledge; pitta and kapha functions; balanced tridoṣa; concluding summary.
- Important source-fidelity rule: preserve the SARIT sequence and unusual editorial spellings exactly rather than silently normalizing them.
- Curriculum boundary: **Chapter 13 onward belongs to SA-2 / Second Professional**, not SA-1.
