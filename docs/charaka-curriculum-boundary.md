## Chapter-build gate

No SA2 chapter should be added to the SA1 content batch merely because it is the next classical chapter number. Before each chapter is merged, verify:

1. NCISM professional-year mapping.
2. Classical Sanskrit sequence against an authoritative/traceable text source.
3. Hindi meaning and original study explanation.
4. Verse-specific टीका.
5. Learning-unit coverage.
6. Assessment coverage with canonical verse references.
7. Reader/API regression tests.

## Chapter 7 research baseline

**Navegāndhāraṇīya Adhyāya (Sūtrasthāna 7)** is the next SA1 content target. The verified text source used for research is Vedic Samhita's SARIT-based Sanskrit edition. Its page records 137 passages, including two prose passages, with the canonical chapter sequence ending at 7.66. The chapter covers non-suppressible natural urges, consequences of suppression, restrainable mental/behavioral impulses, exercise, gradual habit change, prakṛti, mala pathways, prevention, sadvṛtta, and the regimen for curd.

Source: https://www.vedicsamhita.in/ayurveda/caraka/1/7
