## Sequence decision
The canonical learning representation contains the opening invocation, two prose opening sūtras, verses 2–48, and the concluding 48½ half-verse. The e-Vāgbhaṭa reader groups material by adhikaraṇa, so repository IDs are passage-based and preserve prose/verse boundaries rather than forcing every UI group into a verse.

## Commentary policy
Two separate Hindi study layers are stored: Sarvāṅgasundarā and Āyurvedarasāyana. They are original Hindi translations/summaries of the commentary meaning, not verbatim reproduction.

## Reader requirements
The existing AaptaKosha Samhita reader already provides browser speech synthesis through the Read button. Chapter 1 uses that generic path; Sanskrit is rendered as the canonical mūla, followed by Hindi meaning and the two commentary layers.
