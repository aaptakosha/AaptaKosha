## Source-sequence verification
The primary source reports **40 passages, 29 in prose**. It presents:
- 8.1 as a numbered verse-like opening passage
- 8.2–8.29 as prose
- 8.30–8.34 as numbered concluding verses
Therefore the canonical model must not assume that every numbered passage is a śloka or silently renumber the prose material.

## Core content sequence
1. Indriya, indriyadravya, indriyādhiṣṭhāna, indriyārtha and indriyabuddhi
2. Mind (manas/sattva/cetas) and its relation to the senses
3. Non-simultaneity and predominance of sattva, rajas and tamas
4. Five senses, their elements, seats, objects and cognitions
5. Adhyātma-dravya-guṇa-saṃgraha and karma
6. Mahābhūta predominance in the senses
7. Atiyoga, ayoga and mithyāyoga of sense-object contact
8. Proper sense use, observation, action and country/time/self factors
9. Sadvṛtta and detailed conduct rules
10. Concluding ślokas on pañcapañcaka, mind, healthy conduct and its benefits

The source page explicitly states that its Sanskrit is the SARIT IAST edition with mechanically transliterated Devanagari and that it does not include an English translation. This makes it suitable for sequence/transcription verification but not sufficient as a Hindi-translation authority by itself.
