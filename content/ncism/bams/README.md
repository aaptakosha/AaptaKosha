# NCISM BAMS Study Content — Curriculum Roadmap

**Project:** AaptaKosha  
**Purpose:** Build topic-wise, exam-oriented study content for BAMS First, Second, and Third Professional, following the applicable NCISM curriculum documents.

## Content rules

- Organize content by professional year → subject → unit/chapter → topic.
- Record the NCISM curriculum source, document/version, subject code, and learning outcome when verified.
- Keep classical source text, translation, explanation, and modern learning aids clearly separated.
- Do not fabricate Sanskrit verses, source citations, subject codes, or syllabus outcomes.
- Include definitions, concept explanations, comparison tables, diagrams where useful, glossary, MCQs with answers, short-answer questions, long-answer prompts, and quick revision.
- Label content needing faculty/source verification instead of presenting uncertain details as authoritative.
- Classical Ayurvedic content is for academic learning; do not turn it into individualized treatment advice.
