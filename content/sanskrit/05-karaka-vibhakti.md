# Sanskrit Chapter 5 — कारक एवं विभक्ति

## Learning objectives

Identify grammatical case endings, connect them with semantic relations, parse Ayurveda sentences, and translate safely.

## 1. कारक

The major कारक relations taught for practical Sanskrit are:

1. कर्तृ
2. कर्म
3. करण
4. सम्प्रदान
5. अपादान
6. अधिकरण

**सम्बन्ध** is commonly discussed with षष्ठी, and **उपपदविभक्ति** must be learned through usage.

## 2. कारक vs विभक्ति

Think of them as two different questions:

- **विभक्ति:** Which grammatical form is present?
- **कारक:** What role does that form play in the action?

Example:
**वैद्यः औषधेन रोगं शमयति।**

- वैद्यः — प्रथमा; कर्तृ
- औषधेन — तृतीया; करण
- रोगम् — द्वितीया; कर्म

## 3. Core mapping

| कारक | Typical विभक्ति | Practical meaning |
|---|---|---|
| कर्तृ | प्रथमा | doer |
| कर्म | द्वितीया | object |
| करण | तृतीया | instrument/means |
| सम्प्रदान | चतुर्थी | recipient/purpose |
| अपादान | पञ्चमी | separation/source |
| अधिकरण | सप्तमी | locus/context |

This is a starting map, not a substitute for contextual grammar.

## 4. Ayurveda examples

### करण
**औषधेन रोगः शम्यते।**  
“By means of the medicine, the disease is alleviated.”

### अधिकरण
**देहे दोषाः वर्तन्ते।**  
“The doṣas are present in the body.”

### अपादान
**रोगात् बलं क्षीयते।**  
“Strength declines because of/from disease.”

### सम्प्रदान
**रोगिणे औषधं दीयते।**  
“Medicine is given to the patient.”

## 5. उपपदविभक्ति

Some words or constructions require a particular case ending because of the governing word. These should be learned as constructions.

For students, this means:
**do not translate a case ending in isolation.**

## 6. Translation algorithm

For an Ayurveda sentence:

**Step 1:** find the finite verb.  
**Step 2:** find the nominative/doer.  
**Step 3:** mark objects.  
**Step 4:** identify instrument, recipient, source and location.  
**Step 5:** resolve sandhi.  
**Step 6:** translate naturally.

### Practice sentence

**वैद्यः रोगिणे हितकरम् औषधं ददाति।**

Parsing:
- वैद्यः — कर्तृ
- रोगिणे — सम्प्रदान
- औषधम् — कर्म
- ददाति — verb

Natural translation:
“The physician gives a beneficial medicine to the patient.”

## Exam focus

Prepare:
- six major कारक definitions
- विभक्ति identification
- Ayurveda-based identification of कारक
- Sanskrit ↔ English translation
- sentence construction

### Common mistake

**Do not write “तृतीया = करण” as an absolute rule.**  
Case and semantic role are related, but the actual sentence decides the interpretation.
