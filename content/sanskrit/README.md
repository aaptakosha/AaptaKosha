# Sanskrit — AyUG-SN & AI (First Professional BAMS)

**NCISM subject:** AyUG-SN & AI — Sanskrit and Ayurveda Itihas  
**Component covered here:** Paper I — Sanskrit  
**Purpose:** A practical, Ayurveda-oriented Sanskrit course for first-professional BAMS students.

## How this course is organised

This content follows the NCISM Sanskrit learning outcomes and turns them into a progressive study path:

1. वर्णमाला, माहेश्वरसूत्र, उच्चारण
2. व्याकरण-संज्ञा and essential technical terms
3. अव्यय and sentence use
4. शब्दरूप and सर्वनाम
5. धातुरूप and basic verb use
6. कारक and विभक्ति
7. सन्धि
8. समास
9. उपसर्ग, प्रत्यय, पर्याय and निरुक्ति
10. छन्द, अलंकार and text-reading skills
11. Ayurveda-text translation and interpretation
12. Sanskrit speaking, writing and revision tools

### Student-first rule

Every chapter contains:
- **NCISM coverage**
- **Concept → example → Ayurveda application**
- tables and pattern charts where useful
- common mistakes
- exam-focused points
- practice questions
- a short revision checklist

### What students should ultimately be able to do

A student should not merely memorise grammar definitions. The practical goal is to open a Sanskrit Ayurveda passage and be able to:

**read → split sandhi → identify word forms → identify case/कारक → identify compounds → recognise verb/root → consult a dictionary → derive the contextual meaning → translate → explain the Ayurvedic concept.**

NCISM specifically expects Sanskrit knowledge to support interpretation of Ayurveda texts and the use of technical terms, nouns/pronouns, verbs, suffixes, grammatical terms, sandhi and compounds.
