# Paper I — Sanskrit (100 Marks)

**NCISM:** AyUG-SN & AI — First Professional BAMS

Paper I is the foundational Sanskrit paper. NCISM lists **11 topics**. The examination is 100 marks: 20 MCQs + 8 short-answer questions (5 marks each) + 4 long-answer questions (10 marks each).

## What students must be able to do

- Read Sanskrit accurately using **उच्चारणस्थान** and **प्रयत्न**.
- Identify grammatical technical terms used in rules.
- Perform **पदच्छेद** and identify sandhi.
- Recognise **समास** and produce an appropriate **विग्रह**.
- Decline prescribed **शब्दरूप** correctly.
- Identify a verb's **धातु, लकार, पुरुष, वचन**.
- Recognise important **प्रत्यय** and understand the derived word.
- Identify **कारक–विभक्ति** relationships.
- Match **विशेषण** with its **विशेष्य**.
- Apply all of the above to Ayurveda terminology and short passages.

## Exam workflow

**देखो → पदच्छेद करो → शब्दरूप/धातुरूप पहचानो → कारक/विभक्ति पहचानो → समास/सन्धि पहचानो → प्रत्यय/उपसर्ग पहचानो → अन्वय बनाओ → अर्थ निकालो**

> The chapter content should always distinguish what is explicitly examinable from background material useful for reading Ayurveda Saṃhitās.
