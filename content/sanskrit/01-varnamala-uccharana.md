# Sanskrit Chapter 1 — वर्णमाला एवं उच्चारण

**NCISM:** वर्णमालापरिचय, माहेश्वरसूत्राणि, उच्चारणस्थानानि, बाह्यप्रयत्नानि, अभ्यन्तरप्रयत्नानि

## Learning objectives

After this chapter, the student should be able to:

1. recognise and pronounce Sanskrit vowels and consonants;
2. distinguish ह्रस्व, दीर्घ and प्लुत;
3. identify the traditional वर्ग arrangement of consonants;
4. understand the practical purpose of the Māheśvara-sūtras;
5. identify major उच्चारणस्थान and प्रयत्न;
6. avoid pronunciation errors that alter meaning in Ayurvedic recitation.

## 1. स्वर

| Category | Letters | Practical note |
|---|---|---|
| ह्रस्व | अ, इ, उ, ऋ, ऌ | short duration |
| दीर्घ | आ, ई, ऊ, ॠ | long duration |
| संयुक्त/विशेष | ए, ऐ, ओ, औ | traditionally treated as long vowels in many teaching contexts |

**Important:** ऋ is a vowel in Sanskrit grammar. Do not pronounce it as the Hindi consonantal “रि” in every context.

## 2. व्यञ्जन

The five traditional वर्ग are:

| वर्ग | Letters | Place |
|---|---|---|
| क-वर्ग | क ख ग घ ङ | कण्ठ |
| च-वर्ग | च छ ज झ ञ | तालु |
| ट-वर्ग | ट ठ ड ढ ण | मूर्धा |
| त-वर्ग | त थ द ध न | दन्त |
| प-वर्ग | प फ ब भ म | ओष्ठ |

The remaining consonants include य र ल व and श ष स ह.

## 3. उच्चारणस्थान

| स्थान | General examples |
|---|---|
| कण्ठ | अ, क-वर्ग, ह |
| तालु | इ, च-वर्ग, य, श |
| मूर्धा | ट-वर्ग, र, ष |
| दन्त | त-वर्ग, ल, स |
| ओष्ठ | उ, प-वर्ग |
| कण्ठतालु | ए, ऐ |
| कण्ठोष्ठ | ओ, औ |
| दन्तोष्ठ | व |

For examination, learn the traditional mapping, but use the table as a pronunciation aid rather than trying to memorise isolated lists.

## 4. अभ्यन्तर प्रयत्न

The internal effort of articulation helps classify consonants according to how the articulatory organs approach one another. Major categories include:

- स्पृष्ट
- ईषत्स्पृष्ट
- ईषद्विवृत
- विवृत
- संवृत

For BAMS, the clinical value is less important than accurate reading and recognition of grammatical descriptions.

## 5. बाह्य प्रयत्न

Important external features include:

- घोष / अघोष
- अल्पप्राण / महाप्राण
- उदात्त / अनुदात्त / स्वरित in the context of accent

### The five वर्ग pattern

Every वर्ग follows a memorable sequence:

**अघोष अल्पप्राण → अघोष महाप्राण → घोष अल्पप्राण → घोष महाप्राण → nasal**

Example: क → ख → ग → घ → ङ

This pattern is extremely useful for learning Sanskrit pronunciation.

## 6. माहेश्वरसूत्राणि

The Māheśvara-sūtras are a traditional ordered set of sound groups used in Pāṇinian grammar to form **प्रत्याहार**.

A commonly taught sequence is:

1. अ इ उ ण्
2. ऋ ऌ क्
3. ए ओ ङ्
4. ऐ औ च्
5. ह य व र ट्
6. ल ण्
7. ञ म ङ ण न म्
8. झ भ ञ्
9. घ ढ ध ष्
10. ज ब ग ड द श्
11. ख फ छ ठ थ च ट त व्
12. क प य्
13. श ष स र्
14. ह ल्

The final marker in each line is an इत् marker; it is not included in the sound represented by the प्रत्याहार.

### Why students need this

You will encounter forms such as **अच्**, **हल्**, **इक्**, **यण्** in Sanskrit grammar and in explanations of sandhi. You do not need to panic when you see them: they are compact sound-group labels.

## 7. उच्चारण mistakes students commonly make

| Incorrect habit | Better approach |
|---|---|
| क and ख treated alike | maintain aspiration distinction |
| त and ट treated alike | dental vs retroflex |
| द and ड treated alike | dental vs retroflex |
| श, ष, स merged | learn their traditional articulation |
| अ and आ duration ignored | consciously maintain vowel length |
| ऋ replaced mechanically by “रि” | learn the Sanskrit sound and word pattern |

## Ayurveda application

Correct pronunciation matters because Sanskrit technical terms are dense with meaning:

- दोष
- धातु
- स्रोतस्
- अग्नि
- ओजस्
- शोधन
- चिकित्सा
- प्रज्ञापराध

A pronunciation error can make a familiar term unfamiliar during oral examination or text reading.
