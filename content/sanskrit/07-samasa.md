# Sanskrit Chapter 7 — समास

## Learning objectives

Identify compound words in Ayurveda texts, expand them using समास-विग्रह, and understand how compounds compress meaning.

## 1. What is समास?

A **समास** is a compact grammatical construction in which multiple words are combined into a single compound form.

Ayurveda uses compounds heavily because technical concepts are expressed economically.

## 2. Main types students should recognise

| Type | Recognition idea |
|---|---|
| अव्ययीभाव | first member is generally an indeclinable element and the whole behaves accordingly |
| तत्पुरुष | relation between members is understood through a case relation |
| कर्मधारय | descriptive/appositional relation |
| द्विगु | numerical first member |
| द्वन्द्व | coordinated members |
| बहुव्रीहि | compound denotes something other than the members themselves |

Different grammatical traditions subdivide these further; learn the NCISM-prescribed classification used by your department.

## 3. विग्रह

**विग्रह** means expanding the compound to reveal the underlying relationship.

Example pattern:

**राजपुरुषः**  
→ राज्ञः पुरुषः  
→ “the king's man”

The exact vigraha must follow grammatical context.

## 4. Ayurveda examples

### त्रिदोष
**त्रयः दोषाः = त्रिदोषाः**  
A compact expression involving three doṣas.

### दिनचर्या
**दिने चर्या = दिनचर्या**  
“daily regimen” in practical contextual interpretation.

### रोगनिदान
**रोगस्य निदानम् = रोगनिदानम्**  
“diagnosis/causal investigation of disease”, depending on context.

### स्वास्थवृत्त
Interpretation requires attention to the intended technical sense, not only literal word-by-word translation.

## 5. How to solve a compound in an exam

Ask:

1. What are the component words?
2. What relation exists between them?
3. Which case relation would appear if separated?
4. Does the whole word denote the members themselves or something else?
5. Which समास category fits?

## 6. Bahuvrihi warning

In a बहुव्रीहि, the compound often describes an external referent.

Therefore, literal member-by-member translation can be misleading.

## 7. Clinical importance

Ayurvedic terminology contains many compounds:

- दोषप्रकोप
- अग्निमान्द्य
- आमवात
- रसधातु
- रोगमार्ग
- निदानपञ्चक
- दिनचर्या
- ऋतुचर्या

A student who can identify compounds reads faster and understands commentary more accurately.
