# Paper II — Sanskrit and Ayurveda Itihas

**NCISM:** AyUG-SN & AI — First Professional BAMS

Paper II is **100 marks total**:
- **Sanskrit — 80 marks** (SAQ + LAQ)
- **Ayurved Itihas — 20 marks** (MCQ)

## Part A — Sanskrit (80 marks)

NCISM lists five major Sanskrit units:

| No. | Unit | Core material |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **निरुक्ति तथा पर्यायपदानि** | Ayurveda-related technical words, their nirukti and synonyms |
| 2 | **परिभाषापदानि** | Core Ayurvedic technical terminology and definitions |
| 3 | **अष्टाङ्गहृदयम्** | Prescribed selected chapters/passages |
| 4 | **महत्त्वपूर्ण आयुर्वेद सुभाषित** | Important Ayurvedic subhāṣita material and interpretation |
| 5 | **पञ्चतन्त्रम्** | Prescribed stories, grammar, translation and language application |

### Aṣṭāṅgahṛdaya prescribed selection

The syllabus/book-aligned selection includes:
1. अध्याय 1 — **आयुष्कामीय / मङ्गलाचरण-आरम्भिक पाठ** (prescribed passage)
2. अध्याय 2 — **दिनचर्या**
3. अध्याय 4 — **रोगानुत्पादनीय**
4. अध्याय 11 — **दोषादिविज्ञानीय**
5. अध्याय 12 — **दोषभेदीय**
6. अध्याय 13 — **दोषोपक्रमणीय**
7. अध्याय 14 — **द्विविधोपक्रमणीय**

For every prescribed passage AaptaKosha should provide:
**श्लोक → पदच्छेद → अन्वय → शब्दार्थ → व्याकरण → भावार्थ → आयुर्वेदिक meaning → exam questions**.

### Panchatantra prescribed stories

The NCISM-aligned material identifies five stories under **अपरिक्षितकारकम्**:
1. नापित-पणक कथा
2. नकुली-ब्राह्मणी कथा
3. चूडाकर कथा
4. सिंहकारक मूर्खपण्डित कथा
5. मूर्खपण्डित कथा

The exact Sanskrit text should be taught from the prescribed source, not reconstructed from memory.
