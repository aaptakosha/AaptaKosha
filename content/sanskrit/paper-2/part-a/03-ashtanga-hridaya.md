# Paper II-A, Topic 3 — अष्टाङ्गहृदयम्

This unit is **text-based Sanskrit learning**. The goal is not merely to memorise Ayurvedic facts; students must be able to read, parse and explain the prescribed Sanskrit.

## How to study every passage

### 1. मूलपाठ
Read the prescribed Sanskrit exactly as given in the authorised text.

### 2. पदच्छेद
Separate sandhied words.

### 3. अन्वय
Rearrange the prose order so the grammatical relationship becomes clear.

### 4. शब्दार्थ
Record:
- सामान्य अर्थ
- Ayurvedic technical meaning
- difficult compounds
- important synonyms

### 5. व्याकरण
Mark:
- विभक्ति and वचन
- धातु and लकार
- उपसर्ग
- प्रत्यय
- सन्धि
- समास
- विशेषण–विशेष्य agreement

### 6. भावार्थ
Write the meaning in clear Hindi/English without changing the Ayurvedic concept.

### 7. Application
Ask: **Where will I meet this concept again in Samhita, Kriya, Rachana, Dravyaguna or Chikitsa?**

## Example of the study format

**दोषा धातुमला मूलं सदा देहस्य** — when studying a line like this, do not memorise only the translation.

Create a table:

| Element | Identify |
|---|---|
| दोषाः | noun, plural |
| धातवः | noun, plural |
| मलाः | noun, plural |
| मूलम् | noun, neuter |
| देहस्य | genitive relation |

Then write the contextual Ayurvedic meaning.

## Examination focus

The paper layout allows Sanskrit SAQ and LAQ. Therefore students should prepare:
- पदच्छेद
- अन्वय
- शब्दार्थ
- भावार्थ
- grammatical identification
- explanation of selected passages
- technical terminology occurring in the prescribed chapters.

> **Important:** AaptaKosha should store the authorised text and commentary separately. Do not silently reconstruct missing verses from an unrelated edition.
