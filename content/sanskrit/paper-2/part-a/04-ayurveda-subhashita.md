# Paper II-A, Topic 4 — महत्त्वपूर्ण आयुर्वेद सुभाषित

## Learning goal

Students should be able to read selected Ayurvedic subhāṣita, understand the Sanskrit construction, explain the intended teaching and connect it to Ayurvedic practice.

For each prescribed subhāṣita, AaptaKosha will provide:

1. **मूल श्लोक**
2. **पदच्छेद**
3. **अन्वय**
4. **शब्दार्थ**
5. **व्याकरण**
6. **भावार्थ**
7. **आयुर्वेदिक तात्पर्य**
8. **प्रयोग / clinical relevance where appropriate**
9. **exam questions**

## What to look for

- आदेशात्मक forms
- technical Ayurvedic vocabulary
- compound words
- sandhi
- important synonyms
- cause–effect statements
- concise principles expressed in verse

## Revision card

For every verse, students should be able to answer:

**कः विषयः? — मुख्य पदानि कानि? — अन्वयः कः? — सरलार्थः कः? — आयुर्वेदिक सिद्धान्तः कः?**

> The final verse list should be populated from the authorised NCISM-prescribed source used by the institution, rather than generated from an unverified quotation list.
