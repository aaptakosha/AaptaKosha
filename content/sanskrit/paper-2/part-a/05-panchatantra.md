## Why Pañcatantra is included

This unit develops practical Sanskrit reading through narrative prose. It is especially useful for learning:
- sentence structure;
- कारक–विभक्ति relationships;
- verb forms;
- अव्यय;
- उपसर्ग;
- sandhi;
- compounds;
- direct and indirect meaning;
- वाच्य प्रयोग.

## How to study a story

### A. Read
Read the complete prescribed passage aloud.

### B. Mark the grammar
For every sentence identify:
- कर्ता
- कर्म
- क्रिया
- important विभक्ति
- धातु
- उपसर्ग
- अव्यय

### C. Parse difficult words
Make a mini vocabulary list with:
**word → form → base word/dhātu → meaning in context**.

### D. Translate
First produce a literal sense; then produce a fluent translation.

### E. Explain
State the story's sequence and central teaching.

## Examination focus

The NCISM assessment guidance connects this text to practical grammar/application, including **वाच्य प्रयोग**. Questions should therefore be practised from the prescribed Panchatantra sentences rather than from unrelated examples.

> **Text integrity rule:** Store the authorised prescribed edition as the source text. Do not replace it with a modern paraphrase and call that the syllabus text.
