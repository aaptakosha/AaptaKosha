## How to study a paribhāṣā

For every term make a five-part card:

**परिभाषा → सरल अर्थ → आयुर्वेदिक context → example → confusing term**

Example structure:

> **गुरु**  
> सरल अर्थ: heavy / difficult to digest in the relevant context.  
> Do not stop at the English gloss; learn the Ayurvedic technical application and the relation to its opposite **लघु**.

## Exam focus

- Exact concept recognition.
- Distinguishing closely related technical terms.
- Applying a definition to a short sentence.
- Writing a concise, technically correct explanation for SAQ/LAQ.
