# Sanskrit Chapter 10 — छन्द, अलंकार एवं पाठ-वाचन

## Learning objectives

Read Ayurvedic verse with confidence, recognise basic poetic structure, and extract meaning without being distracted by metre or ornament.

## 1. पद्य and गद्य

Ayurvedic texts use both:

- **गद्य** — prose
- **पद्य** — verse

Verse compresses information. Therefore, the grammatical order may not match the order expected in a modern Indian language.

## 2. छन्द

**छन्द** concerns metrical structure.

For first-professional reading, the practical goals are:

- recognise that a verse has a metrical structure;
- count syllabic units when required by the prescribed teaching;
- maintain correct pronunciation and rhythm;
- avoid treating metre as a substitute for grammar.

### Common metre students encounter

**अनुष्टुभ्/श्लोक** is especially important in classical Sanskrit literature.

A traditional śloka commonly has four pādas, with approximately eight syllabic positions per pāda in the familiar anuṣṭubh pattern.

For exams, follow the exact rules taught by your department rather than relying on approximate counting.

## 3. अलंकार

An **अलंकार** is a poetic figure that enhances expression.

At BAMS level, the most important skill is recognition and appreciation rather than excessive literary theory.

Common broad examples:
- उपमा — simile
- रूपक — metaphorical identification
- अनुप्रास — sound repetition/alliteration

## 4. Reading an Ayurvedic śloka

Use this sequence:

**Step 1:** recite accurately.  
**Step 2:** identify sandhi.  
**Step 3:** perform पदच्छेद.  
**Step 4:** identify word forms.  
**Step 5:** identify verb(s).  
**Step 6:** identify compounds.  
**Step 7:** rearrange into prose order if necessary.  
**Step 8:** translate.  
**Step 9:** explain the Ayurveda concept.

## 5. पदच्छेद

Padded reading is not enough. A student must learn to separate a continuous verse into meaningful words.

Example workflow:

**संहितारूप → पदच्छेद → अन्वय → अर्थ**

## 6. अन्वय

**अन्वय** rearranges the words into a syntactically clearer order, especially in verse.

It is one of the most useful bridges between Sanskrit verse and understanding.

## 7. Practical study template

For every prescribed verse, make five lines:

1. **मूलपाठ**
2. **पदच्छेद**
3. **अन्वय**
4. **शब्दार्थ**
5. **भावार्थ / Ayurvedic significance**

This format is ideal for viva, written answers and later Samhita study.

## Practice

Take one verse from your prescribed Samhita and prepare the five-line template above.

### Exam focus

Know the difference between:
- गद्य and पद्य
- पदच्छेद and अन्वय
- छन्द and अलंकार

### Core skill

**Pronunciation + grammar + context = meaningful recitation.**
