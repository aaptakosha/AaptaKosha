# Sanskrit Chapter 6 — सन्धि

## Learning objectives

Recognise common sandhi forms in Samhitas, split them correctly, and identify the rule responsible.

## 1. What is सन्धि?

**सन्धि** is the phonetic/grammatical combination that occurs when sounds meet.

In reading Ayurveda texts, sandhi is one of the first barriers between the printed form and the form you need for dictionary lookup.

### Golden rule

**Never search an unfamiliar sandhied word in a dictionary before trying to split it.**

## 2. Main categories

For first-professional practical work, focus on:

- अच्/स्वर सन्धि
- यण् सन्धि
- गुण सन्धि
- वृद्धि सन्धि
- अयादि/अयवायाव-related forms as prescribed
- later, व्यञ्जन and विसर्ग patterns encountered in text

## 3. यण् सन्धि

A common rule:

**इको यणचि**

When the relevant इ/ई, उ/ऊ, ऋ/ॠ-type vowels meet a following vowel, corresponding semivowel behaviour can appear.

Common practical correspondences:

इ/ई → य्  
उ/ऊ → व्  
ऋ → र्

Students should learn the rule with examples and then practise splitting.

## 4. गुण सन्धि

A common pattern:

- अ/आ + इ/ई → ए
- अ/आ + उ/ऊ → ओ
- अ/आ + ऋ → अर्

This is why an apparently unfamiliar ए or ओ may represent two original vowels.

## 5. वृद्धि सन्धि

- अ/आ + ए/ऐ → ऐ
- अ/आ + ओ/औ → औ

## 6. Practical sandhi-viccheda workflow

When you see an unfamiliar form:

1. mark suspicious vowel/consonant junctions;
2. try likely sandhi rules;
3. test whether both resulting words are valid;
4. check grammar;
5. check the sentence meaning;
6. only then consult a dictionary/commentary.

## 7. Why sandhi matters in Ayurveda

A Samhita may present:

**देहोऽयम्**

You must recognise:
**देहः + अयम्**

Similarly, punctuation is not a reliable substitute for grammatical analysis.
