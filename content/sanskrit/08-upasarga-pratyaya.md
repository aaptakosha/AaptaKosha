# Sanskrit Chapter 8 — उपसर्ग एवं प्रत्यय

## Learning objectives

Understand how prefixes and suffixes alter or generate Sanskrit words and use derivation to interpret Ayurveda terminology.

## 1. उपसर्ग

Common उपसर्ग:

**प्र, परा, अप, सम्, अनु, अव, नि, निर्/निस्, दु-/दुर्, वि, आ, अधि, अति, सु, उद्/उत्, अभि, प्रति, परि, उप**

### Important principle

The meaning of an उपसर्ग is **context-dependent**. It modifies the verbal or lexical meaning; it is not always a fixed English word.

Examples built around गम्:

- आगमन
- निर्गमन
- उपगमन
- प्रतिगमन
- प्रगमन

The root remains recognisable even when the final meaning changes.

## 2. प्रत्यय

A **प्रत्यय** is an affix added according to grammatical derivation.

For BAMS reading, students should understand two broad practical ideas:

- forms generated from verbal roots;
- forms generated from nominal bases/other stems.

Do not memorise suffixes as isolated lists. Always connect:

**धातु/प्रकृति → प्रत्यय → derived word → contextual meaning**

## 3. Ayurveda examples

Common technical words should be analysed through a dictionary and reliable grammatical source rather than invented etymologies.

Examples:
- पाचन
- शोधन
- सेवन
- ग्रहण
- धारण
- चिकित्सा
- निदान
- करण
- वमन
- विरेचन

The useful student question is:
**“What does this form tell me about the action or concept?”**

## 4. उपसर्ग + धातु + प्रत्यय

A useful conceptual model:

**उपसर्ग + धातु + प्रत्यय → पद**

Not every Sanskrit word contains all three.

## 5. Why derivation helps Ayurveda

Technical terminology often preserves conceptual relationships. Understanding derivation helps you distinguish:

- action from agent,
- process from result,
- quality from substance,
- cause from effect.

But derivation should support—not replace—the actual textual meaning.

## 6. Exam method

When asked for निरुक्ति/व्युत्पत्ति:

1. write the accepted base/root;
2. state the relevant affix/prefix if known;
3. give the literal derivational sense;
4. give the contextual Ayurveda meaning;
5. avoid unsupported folk etymology.

## Practice

1. Define उपसर्ग.
2. Give ten common उपसर्ग.
3. Explain why a prefix cannot always be translated by one English word.
4. Explain the role of प्रत्यय.
5. Analyse three Ayurveda technical words with a standard Sanskrit/Ayurveda dictionary.

### Exam tip

If uncertain about a derivation, **do not manufacture a Sanskrit rule**. State the attested meaning and verify the derivation from a standard कोश or व्याकरण reference.
