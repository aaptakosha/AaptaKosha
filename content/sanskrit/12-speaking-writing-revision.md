# Sanskrit Chapter 12 — Speaking, Writing & Examination Skills

## Learning objectives

Speak simple Sanskrit, write short sentences, summarise a passage, and prepare efficiently for practical/viva examination.

## 1. Sentence building

Start with safe patterns.

### Nominal sentence
**अयं ग्रन्थः प्राचीनः अस्ति।**  
This text is ancient.

### Simple action
**वैद्यः रोगिणं परीक्षते।**  
The physician examines the patient.

### Purpose
**स्वास्थ्याय हितकरम् आहारं सेवेत।**  
One should take wholesome food for health.

Do not begin with ornate Sanskrit. Accuracy comes first.

## 3. Summary writing

A good Sanskrit summary should preserve:

1. topic
2. principal statement
3. important supporting point
4. conclusion

Do not copy every sentence from the original.

## 4. Viva strategy

If asked to explain a grammatical form:

**Name → definition → example → Ayurveda application**

Example:
“तृतीया विभक्ति?”  
Answer structure:
- definition/use
- one normal example
- one Ayurveda example

## 5. Translation strategy in viva

If you do not know a word immediately:

1. identify its ending;
2. identify whether it is noun/verb/indeclinable;
3. look for a familiar root;
4. use sentence context;
5. give a cautious answer.

Avoid inventing meanings.

## 6. 30-minute daily practice

**10 min:** read aloud one verse/prose passage  
**5 min:** sandhi-viccheda  
**5 min:** word-form parsing  
**5 min:** dictionary/technical vocabulary  
**5 min:** translate or summarise

Consistency is more useful than memorising grammar for one night.

## 7. Revision cards

Create cards for:

- विभक्ति endings
- common शब्दरूप
- common धातुरूप
- उपसर्ग
- sandhi rules
- samāsa types
- Ayurveda technical terms
- common अव्यय
- common synonyms

## 8. Final exam checklist

Before the exam, you should be able to:

- read Sanskrit without skipping words;
- pronounce common Ayurveda terms correctly;
- identify common विभक्ति forms;
- identify common धातु forms;
- perform basic sandhi-viccheda;
- recognise major samāsa types;
- identify उपसर्ग and common प्रत्यय;
- use a Sanskrit dictionary;
- explain Ayurveda terminology;
- translate short Sanskrit sentences;
- present पदच्छेद, अन्वय, शब्दार्थ and भावार्थ.

## The master formula

**Read → Split → Parse → Relate → Translate → Explain**

That is the Sanskrit skill-set needed for successful BAMS Samhita study.
