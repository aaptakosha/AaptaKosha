Classification of Drugs

Medicinal substances can be understood through different systems of classification. These systems help learners organise properties, actions and applications.

☘
Learning objective

By the end of this topic, you should be able to explain the major principles used to classify dravyas.

Why classification matters

Classification provides a structured way to connect individual dravyas with their qualities, actions and therapeutic use. It also makes revision easier by grouping related concepts.

01SourceUnderstand where a dravya originates.
02PropertiesConnect qualities with observed effects.
03ActionRelate action to therapeutic application.
✦
RememberThink in relationships, not isolated facts.

When revising, connect source → properties → action → use.
